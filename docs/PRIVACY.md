# Privacy policy

NewspapeRSS has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting. It doesn't
collect anything about you, and nothing you do in it is sent to its makers
or anyone else.

## What stays on your phone

Everything the app keeps is stored on your phone: the sites you follow, the
links you save, your settings, the articles it fetches and the editions it
makes. Uninstalling the app deletes it all.

If you use a Tiny Tiny RSS server, its password is stored encrypted, with a
key kept in your phone's secure storage. Android's backups leave the
password out, so a restored phone asks you to sign in again.

## What the app connects to

The app only connects to:

- **The sites you read:** the sites you add or pick from the starter packs,
  the article pages it fetches for an edition, and the pictures in those
  articles (including the still image of a YouTube video an article
  embeds). These sites see a request from your phone, as they would in a
  browser.
- **Your own Tiny Tiny RSS server,** if you use one, with the login you
  give it. NewspapeRSS's makers never see it.
- **GitHub, in test builds only:** a debug build from the project's own
  builds asks GitHub at most every three hours whether a newer one is out.
  GitHub sees the request from your phone. Release builds never ask.

## Sending editions

Editions go where you send them: through an app you pick from Android's
share sheet, by email from your own mail app (for Send to Kindle), or to a
folder you choose. NewspapeRSS doesn't send them anywhere itself.
