Edition buttons, streamlined

Today's edition card for every device and state, as it is now and as proposed. The edition and headlines are sample data. The ⋮ line under each card lists what that card's menu holds.

Colours:

The proposal in four rules

  • One filled button per card: the next step. That's Send for every device that sends; the name changes only where the action does: Read now on a Boox with the app on it, Open Kindle once sent.
  • The card opens the contents. No "See what's inside" button; a card with no button ends in a link, "and 5 more ›".
  • Everything rare is in ⋮, on every card: open on this phone, send another way, send again, mark as not sent.
  • One short line replaces the paragraph and "I've sent it": "Sent it another way? Mark as sent."

Beyond the card

  • The edition page gets the same button and the same ⋮ as the card, so the two screens never disagree. Its ⋮ adds Delete edition.
  • Boox: the app checks whether it runs on a Boox (Android reports the maker as Onyx). On the Boox it offers Read now; on a phone it offers Send, so opening a copy on the phone can't mark an edition as sent when it never reached the Boox.
  • Notifications already carry one action per device (Send, or Open on a Boox); the same names would carry over.

To check before building

  • A ⋮ on a ready card is one more thing on every card. Most readers never open it.
  • "Mark as sent" as a link in a sentence is easier to miss than today's button. Most sends are counted on their own, but it needs a TalkBack pass.
  • Send doesn't say where the edition goes. If people are unsure, the line under it can name the route ("Goes to your Kobo through Dropbox") without making the button longer.
