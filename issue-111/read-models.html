Read and Unread Models

Read and unread: four models

The same six articles from a source's page, shown four ways. Each model comes from how familiar apps treat read and unread, and asks the same thing differently: is "sent to my Kindle" a kind of read, a place, a step, or just a line of history?

The six articles, as the app knows them today:

  • a waiting article, and a starred one going in the next edition
  • one in Thursday's edition, made but not sent yet
  • one sent in Wednesday's edition
  • one you marked read, and one that got too old to pick

My pick: B now, A later if you don't miss the tick

B is today's toggle with the two-questions idea applied: the mark answers "have I read it?", and the line says what happened. It's a small change. A is the plainest and most familiar from reader apps, but loses the at-a-glance "what reached my Kindle". C and D are bigger rethinks; D is the most honest about what delivery means, but adds a step nobody takes on e-ink.

  • Costs: asks for a confirmation most people never give, so ◐ piles up. The Kindle doesn't tell the app what you read, so the app can't fill it in.

The same in every model

  • ★ stays separate: "put it in my next edition".
  • An article in an edition that hasn't been sent can't change.
  • The data underneath doesn't need to change for A or B. C needs only a different list layout. D needs a "confirmed read" flag.
